CDS Backup

CDS Backup is a private tool used only by its owner to store encrypted backup copies of the owner's own Creative Digital Studio project files in the owner's own Google Drive. It is not offered to the public.

Privacy policy

What it accesses. CDS Backup uses the Google Drive permission drive.file, which allows it to see and manage only the files and folders it creates itself. It cannot see, read or change any other files in your Google Drive.

What it stores. Backup archives of the owner's own project files, encrypted before upload, plus lists of file checksums used to verify the backups.

Sharing. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Files stay in the owner's Google Drive.

Removing access. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions by removing "CDS Backup". Backup files can be deleted from Google Drive like any other file.

Contact. Use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app.
